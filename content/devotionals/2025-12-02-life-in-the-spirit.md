**Scripture Reading:** Romans 8:1–17

> The mind governed by the flesh is death, but the mind governed by the Spirit is life and peace. Romans 8:6

Today’s passage opens with one of the most liberating truths in Scripture: in Christ, there is no condemnation. The Spirit of life sets us free from sin and death, empowering us to live not according to the flesh but according to the Spirit. As we walk in this Spirit-led freedom, our minds and desires are reshaped to reflect God’s heart, moving us from a life centered on earthly cravings to one anchored in divine purpose.

Here are two truths from this passage:

- No condemnation in Christ. In Christ, sin is condemned, and believers are declared righteous. Freedom from condemnation is the cornerstone of our new life in Christ. Because of this profound freedom, our lives are no longer governed by the defeat of the past or the weight of guilt. Now we can approach the throne of Grace boldly trusting in the grace of Jesus Christ. This transformation leads us into a relationship with God that is intimate and secure, where fear is replaced with the assurance of being His beloved children.

- The Spirit gives life and peace. The mind governed by the flesh leads to death, but the Spirit brings life and peace. As we yield to the Spirit daily, the life of God is formed in us, and the peace of God permeates our heart. This Spirit-led life is characterized not by striving or self-effort, but by a steady reliance on God’s presence and power within us. This daily surrender to the Spirit’s leading shapes us into people who radiate Christ’s peace. This peace is based on the confidence we have in our adoption into the family of God. We are heirs of God and co-heirs with Christ.

Today’s passage reminds us that the Christian life is not lived in condemnation but in freedom. The Spirit transforms our minds, secures our identity, and assures us of eternal inheritance. We are not defined by sin but by sonship, not by fear but by faith, not by death but by life in Christ.

**Pray with me:** Lord, thank You that there is no condemnation in Christ. Please help me to yield daily to Your Spirit.
