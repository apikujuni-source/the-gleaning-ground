**Scripture Reading:** Romans 4:1–25

> “Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness.” Romans 4:3

In today’s passage, Paul uses the example of Abraham, to further explain that righteousness has always come by faith, not by works or the law. Abraham was declared righteous before he was circumcised, proving that faith, not ritual or heritage, is the true basis of God’s promise. His unwavering trust in God’s power, even when circumstances seemed impossible, becomes the pattern for all who believe in Christ.

Here are two truths from today’s passage:

- Faith, not works, brings righteousness. Abraham was not justified by his deeds but by believing God’s promise. Paul emphasizes that Abraham was declared righteousness because of His Faith in God even before he was circumcised and long before the law was given. This underscores that God’s acceptance is grounded in our faith in Him rather than our works. Righteousness is credited, not earned. Abraham did not earn God’s acceptance through religious works or moral achievements; instead, he received it as a free gift by simply trusting in God’s promise.

- Faith trusts God against all odds. Paul further used Abraham’s life to explain what faith in God really means. Abraham shows unwavering confidence in God’s promise, despite the impossibility of his situation. This demonstrates the essence of true faith. It is trusting in God’s faithfulness beyond human limitations. Genuine faith looks beyond the natural to the supernatural power of God. Faith is not merely believing in spite of the odds, but embracing God’s promises with assurance, knowing He is able to fulfill what He has said.

Abraham’s story reminds us that faith is not blind optimism but confident trust in God’s power and promises. Just as righteousness was credited to him, it is credited to us when we believe in Christ. Faith is the bridge between human weakness and divine strength. Through faith, a broken human receives access to the unfathomable depth of God’s power. Our faith is assured because it is placed in God.

**Pray with me:** Dear Lord, please strengthen my faith like Abraham’s. Help me trust Your promises even when circumstances seem impossible.
