**Scripture Reading:** Romans 9:19–33

> “What if God, although choosing to show his wrath and make his power known, bore with great patience the objects of his wrath—prepared for destruction? What if he did this to make the riches of his glory known to the objects of his mercy, whom he prepared in advance for glory…” Romans 9:22–23

Paul unveils a profound mystery: God’s sovereign patience and mercy. Even as God allows His justice to be displayed, He does so with great patience, so that His mercy might shine all the brighter. His plan includes not only Israel but also Gentiles. He calls those once “not His people” to become children of the living God. This passage reminds us that salvation is not about human effort but about God’s mercy, and His purposes always stand.

Here are three truths from today’s passage:

- God’s patience magnifies His mercy. Though He could swiftly judge, God bears patiently with those opposed to Him. This patience highlights the riches of His mercy toward those He has called. God’s forbearance isn’t weakness but a deliberate act to display His mercy. Through His longsuffering, we come to understand the depth of His compassion and the certainty that His mercy reaches further than judgment.

- God’s mercy extends to all. Through Hosea and Isaiah, Paul shows that God’s plan was always bigger than Israel alone. He calls those who were once “not His people” to be His beloved and preserves a remnant by His grace. The fulfillment of this is seen in Christ. God brings us, who were once far off, and makes us participants in the covenant promises.

- God’s purpose never fails. Even when Israel stumbled, God’s promises stood firm. His sovereign plan ensures that His mercy is revealed, His glory displayed, and His people preserved. In every generation, God demonstrates that His intentions cannot be thwarted, and His love endures beyond our failures.

Today’s passage reminds us that God’s patience is purposeful, His mercy is expansive, and His promises are unshakable. We are vessels of mercy, prepared for glory, not because of our works, but because of His sovereign grace.

**Pray with me:** Lord, thank You for Your patience and mercy. Help me not to take for granted Your mercy.
