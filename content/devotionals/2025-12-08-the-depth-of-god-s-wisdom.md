**Scripture Reading:** Romans 11:25–36

> “Oh, the depth of the riches of the wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable his judgments, and his paths beyond tracing out!” — Romans 11:33

After wrestling with the mystery of God’s sovereign mercy toward Jew and Gentile, Paul bursts into praise, declaring the unfathomable depth of God’s wisdom, knowledge, and judgments. He reminds us that God’s ways are beyond human comprehension, and all things exist from Him, through Him, and for Him.

Here are three truths from today’s passage

- God’s wisdom is unfathomable. Paul marvels at the depth of God’s wisdom and knowledge. His judgments are unsearchable, His paths beyond tracing out. We cannot fully grasp His purposes, but we can trust them. Even when life’s circumstances leave us perplexed, God remains perfectly wise and good. Even when His ways are mysterious, we are called to deepen our trust, finding rest in the assurance that His plans far exceed our understanding.

- God is self-sufficient and sovereign. God stands utterly independent and self-sufficient, needing nothing from anyone. His wisdom and provision are complete in themselves. We cannot advise Him, nor can we put Him in our debt because every gift and blessing we enjoy springs from His generosity, not our merit. He is the source of all wisdom and the sustainer of all creation.

- All things exist for His glory. “For from him and through him and for him are all things.” Everything begins with God, is sustained by God, and ultimately points back to God’s glory. Every aspect of creation finds its purpose in displaying God’s glory. This awareness moves us beyond mere understanding to wholehearted devotion, inviting us to live each day in awe of God’s wisdom, sovereignty, and grace.

Today’s passage shifts our posture from questioning to worship. When faced with mystery, the right response is not frustration but adoration. God’s wisdom is deeper than our understanding, His sovereignty greater than our plans, and His glory the ultimate purpose of all things.

**Pray with me:** Dear Lord, Your wisdom and knowledge are beyond measure. Help me to trust Your unsearchable ways and live for Your glory.
