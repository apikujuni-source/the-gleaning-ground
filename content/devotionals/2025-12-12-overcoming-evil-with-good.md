**Scripture Reading:** Romans 12:14–21

> “Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.” Romans 12:21

Paul continues to unfold what a redeemed life looks like. Because we have received mercy through Christ, our lives must reflect a new way of responding to the world around us. Christ’s redemption reshapes how we handle hostility, conflict, and injustice. Instead of reacting from the old nature, we respond from the new life we have received, reflecting Christ’s character in difficult situations.

Here are Three truths from today’s passage:

- Redemption reshapes our reactions. Those redeemed by Christ respond differently to mistreatment. Instead of cursing, we bless. Instead of withdrawing, we share in both the positive and challenging experiences of others. Redemption produces a posture of humility and harmony. This transformation empowers us to break cycles of hostility, choosing instead to embody empathy and understanding even in the face of wrongdoing.

- Redemption calls us to pursue peace. Because Christ has made peace with us, we seek peace with others. We do what is right before all people and, as far as it depends on us, live at peace. This is not weakness. It is the strength of a redeemed heart. This peace does not mean avoiding hard conversations or ignoring injustice, but rather engaging others with a spirit of reconciliation and grace.

- Redemption frees us from revenge. We no longer carry the burden of vengeance. Christ’s redemption teaches us to trust God’s justice. Instead of repaying evil, we respond with good. Living out this freedom from retaliation, we become conduits of compassion, reflecting the grace we ourselves have received.

Today’s passage shows that our redemption is reflected in the way we live. Christ’s work in us produces a new way of responding to the world, by overcoming evil with good. As we walk in this new way, our daily choices become living evidence of Christ’s redeeming power. Each act of kindness, forgiveness, and humility demonstrates the reality of God’s grace at work within us.

**Pray with me:** Dear Lord, thank You for redeeming me through Christ. Please teach me daily to overcome evil with the good.
