**Scripture Reading:** Romans 12:1-2

> “Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind.” Romans 12:2

In today’s passage, Paul calls believers not to be shaped by the world’s values but to be transformed by God’s Spirit. Transformation is not external behavior modification, it begins in the mind, reshaping how we think, perceive, and discern. This renewal enables us to walk in God’s good, pleasing, and perfect will.

Here are three truths from today’s passage:

- Reject conformity to the world. The world pressures us to fit its mold. Instead of yielding to these pressures, we are called to let God’s truth shape our identity and choices, allowing His Spirit to redefine what we pursue and value. As we surrender each day, our lives become evidence of God’s transformative work.

- Embrace renewal of the mind. Transformation begins with God’s Spirit reshaping our thoughts, desires, and priorities. A renewed mind sees life through the lens of Christ. As we allow the Spirit to renew our minds, our perspective shifts from temporary pursuits to eternal values, empowering us to make wise decisions that align with God’s heart.

- Discern God’s perfect will. When our minds are renewed by God's Spirit, we develop spiritual discernment that enables us to recognize and embrace His will with joy and confidence. Living in this renewed perspective, we can confidently step into each day knowing that God’s guidance leads us to what is truly best, shaping us more into the image of Christ and allowing His love to flow through us to the world.

Today’s passage invites us to a life of active surrender to God’s Spirit. This transformation is a continual process. Each day presents new opportunities to submit our minds to God and to allow His truth to displace the lies and distractions that compete for our attention. As we cultivate this daily renewal, we begin to see lasting change not only in our thoughts but also in our relationships, choices, and character.

**Pray with me:** Dear Lord, please transform my thoughts, desires, and actions so that I may walk in Your perfect will.
