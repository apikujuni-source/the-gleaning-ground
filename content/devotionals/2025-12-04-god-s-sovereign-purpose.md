**Scripture Reading:** Romans 9:1–18

> “It does not, therefore, depend on human desire or effort, but on God’s mercy.” Romans 9:16

Paul opens this chapter with deep anguish for Israel, his own people. Though they were entrusted with adoption, covenants, promises, and even the ancestry of Christ, many rejected the Messiah. Yet Paul insists that God’s word has not failed. Salvation is not based on human effort or heritage but on God’s sovereign choice. His mercy and compassion are freely given, not earned, and His purposes stand firm.

Here are three truths from this passage:

- God’s promises never fail. Throughout history, God’s word has proven steadfast despite human shortcomings. God's steadfast dedication to His word gives us confidence that anyone who relies on His promises is embraced as part of His family. This is not by merit or ancestry, but by grace. Because of this unwavering faithfulness, we can be rest assured that God’s plan will unfold perfectly, even when circumstances seem uncertain or our understanding is limited.

- Election is rooted in God’s purpose. Even before any action was taken by Jacob or Esau, God’s purpose was already unfolding, affirming that His redemptive plan operates independently of our striving. This compels us to acknowledge that the story of salvation centers on God’s faithful and unchanging character.

- Mercy depends on God, not human effort. Paul reminds us that God’s mercy is not earned by desire or effort but flows from His sovereign will: declares, “I will have mercy on whom I have mercy.” God’s mercy operates according to His wisdom and purpose, not our own qualifications or efforts.

This passage encourages us to reflect on the nature of God's sovereignty and the assurance it brings. When we recognize that our place in God's family is not by our own qualification but by His gracious invitation, we find freedom from striving and a deeper sense of belonging. Our response should be one of gratitude, humility, and trust, knowing that His mercy is freely given and His purposes are always for our good.

**Pray with me:** Lord, thank You that Your word never fails. Help me to trust Your sovereign purpose and rest in Your grace.
