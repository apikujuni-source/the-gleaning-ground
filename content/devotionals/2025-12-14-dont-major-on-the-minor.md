**Scripture Reading:** Romans 14:1–23

> Accept the one whose faith is weak, without quarreling over disputable matters. 2 One person’s faith allows them to eat anything, but another, whose faith is weak, eats only vegetables.... Romans 14:1–3

In today’s passage, Apostle Paul addresses a subtle but destructive tendency within the church: elevating secondary issues to primary importance. In Rome, believers were dividing over food, sacred days, and personal convictions, matters that were never meant to define fellowship. Paul’s message is clear: redeemed people must not major on the minor. When Christ is the center, disputable matters lose their power to divide. Our focus shifts from winning arguments to honoring the Lord and protecting one another’s faith.

Here are three truths from today’s passage:

- Minor issues must never overshadow the Lordship of Christ. Some believers felt free to eat anything; others abstained. Some honored certain days; others treated all days alike. We should never let these differences become dividing lines. Instead, we are called to see one another through the lens of redemption, recognizing each believer as someone Christ has bought and dearly loves. True fellowship is not built on uniformity in minor practices, but on the shared foundation of Christ’s lordship over our lives.

- Redemption reminds us that we live unto the Lord. Our primary calling is to live unto the Lord. Our lives are not our own. Christ’s death and resurrection established His lordship over us all. This reality reframes every disagreement: every believer is someone Christ purchased, someone who will give an account to Him.

- Redemption calls us to protect the faith of others. Freedom is never a license to harm a brother or sister. If our choices distress or weaken another believer, we are no longer walking in the way Christ redeemed us to walk. Even when we are convinced of our freedom, we must consider how our choices affect others. As Christians, we are to pursue what builds up, not what tears down, because we are part of one body of Christ.

Today’s passage calls us to clarity on what truly matters. Christ is central. His lordship is ultimate. His people are precious. Everything else is secondary. Christ has freed us, but He has also made us stewards of one another’s faith. We honor Him not by enforcing our preferences or flaunting our freedoms, but by living with a conscience shaped by His lordship and a posture that protects the spiritual well-being of others.

**Pray with me:** Dear Lord, thank You for redeeming me and making me Yours. Teach me to walk in a way that strengthens my brothers and sisters in the faith.
